1. UNITS AND CONVENTIONS
------------------------

- Load: tonnes (t).
- Length and radius: metres (m).
- Wind speed: kilometres per hour (km/h).
- Radius convention: hook radius, measured from the slewing centre. A radius
  greater than the installed jib is outside the chart.
- Page references are PDF page indices, 1-based. The source PDFs have no printed
  page numbers.
- The MCT 88 capacity is a published chart lookup. It is not calculated with an
  inverse-radius moment formula, so no moment or percentage-error calculation is
  used in this document.


2. MCT 88 CAPACITY - PUBLISHED 5 T HOOK CHART
----------------------------------------------

The MCT 88 uses the upper 5 t hook table on PDF p. 4. The chart has 8 jib rows
(20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50 and 52 m) and 86 published points. The lower table
on the same page is the 3 t hook table and is not used.

Lookup rules implemented by `capacityAt`:

- The installed jib must be exactly one of the eight published configurations.
  For an unsupported jib, `capacityAt` returns null; the pick layer reports
  INVALID with issue `jib-outside-published-range`.
- The printed minimum radius is 2.7 m. A radius below 2.7 m is OUT_OF_RANGE;
  the 5 t plateau does not extend below the published minimum.
- From 2.7 m to the first stored point, the 5 t plateau applies. The first stored
  point is the end of the plateau, not the minimum radius.
- Between two stored points, use the capacity at the next larger radius (the
  lower bounding value). No interpolation is used.
- Beyond the last stored radius, there is no value and the result is null. No
  extrapolation is used.
- The optional SIMULATED wind derate is applied once after the chart lookup; see
  section 6. It is not part of the published chart.

3. MCT 88 STEP-LOOKUP HAND CHECKS
---------------------------------

The requested radius is not snapped to a chart point. The engine returns the
capacity at the next larger published radius, which is conservative because the
chart never increases with radius.

Worked examples:

    28.43 m on the 52 m jib
      published points around it: 27.0 m -> 2.45 t, 30.0 m -> 2.15 t
      next larger radius: 30.0 m
      result: 2.15 t

    39.20 m on the 52 m jib
      published points around it: 37.0 m -> 1.70 t, 40.0 m -> 1.55 t
      next larger radius: 40.0 m
      result: 1.55 t

    47.20 m on the 52 m jib
      published points around it: 47.0 m -> 1.25 t, 50.0 m -> 1.15 t
      next larger radius: 50.0 m
      result: 1.15 t

    31.0 m on the 35 m jib
      published points around it: 29.1 m -> 2.50 t, 31.6 m -> 2.50 t
      next larger radius: 31.6 m
      result: 2.50 t

    31.7 m on the 35 m jib
      published points around it: 31.6 m -> 2.50 t, 32.0 m -> 2.45 t
      next larger radius: 32.0 m
      result: 2.45 t

These checks show that a requested radius between points is not interpolated.
For example, 28.43 m is not reported as a value between 2.45 t and 2.15 t; it is
reported as 2.15 t.

The catalogue's `tipLoadByJib` values are the last published point of each upper
5 t chart row: 20 m -> 3.95 t, 25 m -> 3.00 t, 30 m -> 2.50 t, 35 m -> 2.22 t,
40 m -> 1.90 t, 45 m -> 1.65 t, 50 m -> 1.40 t, 52 m -> 1.10 t.


4. MCT 88 SOURCE DISCREPANCIES
------------------------------

4.1 Two hook tables on p. 4

    Upper table: 5 t hook chart.
      30 m jib tip: 30 m -> 2.50 t
      52 m jib tip: 52 m -> 1.10 t

    Lower table: 3 t hook chart.
      30 m jib tip: 30 m -> 2.70 t
      52 m jib tip: 52 m -> 1.15 t

The catalogue uses the upper 5 t table for all eight supported jib
configurations. The lower 3 t table is not used for capacity or tip-load values,
and points from the two tables must not be mixed.

The p. 1 summary labels do not all equal the upper table (for example, p. 1
labels the 30 m jib at 2.70 t and the 52 m jib at 1.15 t). The catalogue follows
the p. 4 upper 5 t table.

4.2 Dashed -0.245 t annotation

A dashed variant annotation on p. 4 reads `-0.245 t`. The source does not
establish that this is a general deduction from every chart value. It is
recorded but not modelled and is not subtracted from the rated capacities.

4.3 Metric load-curve page

The local 8-page MCT 88 PDF places the metric load curves on p. 4; the catalogue
records p. 4 in `datasheetLoadCharts.page`. The Cycle 2 mirrored copy recorded
the page as p. 3; that number remains unverified against the local copy and is
not used.

4.4 Reference PDF availability

The MCT 88 PDF is present in this repository's working tree at
`plugins/tower-planner/docs/02D_FEM1001mt_MCT88_2006_40-21.pdf`. It is ignored by
Git and untracked; it is not absent. Its SHA-256 is
0B7A7DFFCC7BB8B5DD54BA69120D1828DF80F0C6FD7EAD7AC0D505EFB0F7B82F. Page 1
confirms 52 m as the maximum working jib, 53.1 m as the overall tip length and
1.2 m as the mast width; page 6 confirms 11.9 m as the counter-jib structural
length and 4.38 t as the variant-A counter-jib mass.

4.5 Unresolved standard attribution

The PDF prints FEM 1.001-A3. The catalogue `standard` field is EN 14439 C25,
associated with the e-catalogue URL. The two attributions are not claimed to be
equivalent and the question remains pending mentor confirmation. This document
does not change the catalogue `standard` field.


5. MCT 85 F5 SPOT CHECKS - SEPARATE FROM THE MCT 88 CHART
---------------------------------------------------------

The MCT 85 F5 uses its own published per-radius chart on PDF p. 3. The chart has
8 jib rows and 86 points, and the printed minimum radius is 2.7 m. The lookup
rules are the same as section 2: exact published jib, 2.7 m minimum, 5 t plateau
to the first stored point, next larger radius between points, and no
extrapolation beyond the last point.

The MCT 85 F5 chart is numerically identical to the MCT 88 p. 4 upper 5 t chart
except at the 25 m jib endpoint: MCT 88 3.00 t, MCT 85 F5 3.02 t. The cranes
still have separate source pages and dimensions; do not mix their tip tables or
component-weight tables.

Source discrepancies and limitations:

- p. 1 labels the 25 m tip load as 3.0 t; p. 3 prints 3.02 t at the last chart
  point. The tip-load table keeps the p. 1 label; the capacity lookup uses the
  p. 3 chart value.
- A dashed variant annotation on the chart page reads `-0.245 t`; the source
  does not establish it as a general deduction, and it is not modelled.
- The chart's first stored radius varies by jib (13.7 m to 16.4 m). That is the
  end of the plateau, not the minimum radius; the 2.7 m minimum is stored
  separately in every chart row.
- The MCT 85 F5 PDF is a commercial document and is not legally binding. It
  states no redistribution licence. Its SHA-256 is recorded in `docs/SOURCES.md`;
  the PDF is ignored by Git and excluded from the distributable plugin.


6. WIND RULE
------------

The optional wind rule is a SIMULATED product rule, not a verified manufacturer
or EN 14439 rule:

    if wind is null/undefined:      no derate
    if wind < 0 or non-finite:      INVALID (invalid-wind)
    if 0 <= wind < 20 km/h:         no derate
    if wind >= 20 km/h:             capacity * 0.8, applied once

The factor is applied once to the final base capacity. It does not alter any
published chart value; both the MCT 88 and MCT 85 F5 base capacities are chart
lookups.
